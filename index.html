VR Controls (Meta Quest)

  • Movement: Hold the Grip button on either controller and drag the air to pull yourself through space.
  • Rotation: Hold both Grip buttons simultaneously and twist/rotate your hands to rotate or reposition the virtual space.
  • Timeline Transport: Point your right controller and squeeze the Trigger on the floating timeline panel located above your left hand to play/pause or scrub through timestamps.

Desktop & Mobile Controls

  • Keyboard: Use W A S D or arrow keys to walk. Hold Shift to sprint.
  • Mouse/Touch: Click and drag to look around. On mobile, use the virtual touch-joystick on the left side to move.
